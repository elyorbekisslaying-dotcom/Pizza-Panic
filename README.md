# Pizza Panic

Serve three pizza orders before the kitchen closes. You have 90 seconds and three lives.

1. Move with WASD or the arrow keys and catch the fresh toppings on the order card.
2. Walk up to the oven on the left. Baking takes three seconds. You can move while it bakes.
3. Approach the oven to pick up the finished pizza, then walk to the pickup counter on the right.
4. Deliver all three orders to win. Dodge bombs and spoiled food throughout the shift.

Press Escape to pause or resume. Extra fresh toppings score points, and each delivered pizza adds 100 points. Your best score is saved in your browser when local storage is available.

Open `index.html` in a desktop browser. No installation or build step is needed.
